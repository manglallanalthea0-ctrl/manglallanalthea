A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To My Favorite Person ♡

Hello, my love! 💗

I am so lucky to have someone like you. Please keep smiling, take care of yourself, and never forget that you mean so much to me. I love and appreciate you! 💗

With all my love,

Your Favorite Person ♡
